Capability

Engineered with uncompromising capability designed to help you maneuver your vehicle through almost any situation.

4-Wheel Steer

All four wheels work together to improve the turning circle and help you make those tight turns.*

CrabWalk®

Drive diagonally at low speeds, with the rear wheels turning at the same angle as the front.*

King Crab

The rear wheels turn two times more than the front, to swing the rear and reposition on a trail.*

Watts to Freedom

Available 0-60 mph in approximately 3.5 seconds* for the 3X SUV.

Extract Mode
